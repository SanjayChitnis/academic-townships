# Academic Townships

A Ministry of Education initiative to create master-planned academic districts integrated with national industrial corridors.

## Overview

Academic Townships shifts away from the model of isolated campuses by co-locating multiple institutions within purpose-built, 500–700 acre districts. Each township is strategically positioned along national industrial corridors to foster deep collaboration between academia and industry.

## Key Features

- **Institutional Autonomy** — Each institution retains full academic independence while operating within the shared township.
- **Shared Infrastructure** — State-of-the-art research labs, incubation centers, and industry R&D clusters are available to all member institutions.
- **Industry Integration** — Direct linkage with national industrial corridors to enhance real-world research applicability and graduate employability.
- **Innovation Ecosystem** — Shared incubation centers and R&D clusters enable cross-institutional collaboration and startup formation.

## Goals

1. Reduce duplication of high-cost research infrastructure across institutions.
2. Strengthen academia–industry partnerships through physical proximity and shared facilities.
3. Improve employability outcomes for students through integrated industry R&D exposure.
4. Accelerate innovation by enabling interdisciplinary collaboration across co-located institutions.

## Township Structure

| Component | Description |
|---|---|
| Academic Zones | Individual campuses for each institution with full autonomy |
| Shared Research Labs | Advanced laboratories accessible to all institutions |
| Incubation Centers | Startup and entrepreneurship support facilities |
| Industry R&D Clusters | Dedicated zones for corporate research and collaboration |
| Common Amenities | Housing, health, recreation, and support services |

## Scale

Each township spans **500–700 acres**, designed to accommodate multiple institutions along with shared facilities and supporting infrastructure.

## Initiative

This project is part of the **Ministry of Education** national policy framework to modernize higher education infrastructure and align it with India's industrial development goals.
